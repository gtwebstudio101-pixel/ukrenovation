Our Method

Renovation Step by Step

Two clear routes depending on what your project needs — no guesswork, no surprises.

A Route A

No Planning Permission

Cosmetic and light renovation work that improves your home without requiring council approval. Fast, focused, and efficient.

  • Interior redecoration & painting
  • Kitchen & bathroom refresh
  • Flooring replacement
  • Electrical & plumbing updates
  • Loft & room conversions
B Route B

Planning Permission Required

Structural and major changes that require council involvement. We handle the full process from design submission to final build.

  • Extensions & additions
  • Structural wall removal
  • New build construction
  • Land & property development
  • Change of use applications
